PART 1: The Goal - Work Smart, Not Hard

You are a chef in a busy kitchen. Unlike standard games, your goal is NOT just to be fast. Your goal is to get the highest possible team score.

How scoring works:

  • (+) Team Goal: You gain points for every dish served.
  • (-) Energy Cost: You LOSE points for every step YOU take.

The AI teammate is there to help. To get a high score, you need to find your rhythm.

PART 3: Tips and Reminders

There is no single "correct" role to play. Your goal is to find the strategy that feels most efficient for you.

  • Play naturally: There is no "right" or "wrong" way to play. The AI is designed to observe your unique style and adapt to you. Don't feel forced to play in a specific way; instead, play in a way that feels most efficient and comfortable for you. The more consistent you are, the better the AI can learn to support you.
  • Observe your partner: When the AI updates, you might notice it changing its behavior. It is trying to match your style to make it easier for you to get a good result. Play along with its changes so you both end up working smoothly together.
  • Always keep the "Movement Cost" in mind: You have to make the choice: "Is it worth running to get that plate, or should I wait for the AI to do it?". Find the balance that maximizes your own score.
