PART 1: Play smart not hard!

You are a chef working with an AI teammate in a busy kitchen. Your goal is not just to move fast: your goal is to help your team get the highest possible score.

How scoring works

  • You gain points when dishes are served.
  • You lose points for every step you take.

This means the best strategy is usually to work efficiently: help serve dishes while avoiding unnecessary movement.

Try to coordinate with your AI teammate instead of doing everything by yourself.
