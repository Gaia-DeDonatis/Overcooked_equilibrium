Human–AI Collaboration in a Collaborative Game Environment

Purpose of the study

The purpose of this study is to investigate how people collaborate with an AI teammate in the game-like Overcooked environment. The study examines how human–AI coordination develops over repeated interaction and how participants experience the collaboration.

What participation involves

Participation takes place in a meeting room and lasts approximately 40 minutes. You will first provide background information, read the instructions, complete a practice round, and answer a short comprehension check. If you pass the comprehension check, you will continue to the main task, where you will play several short rounds of the Overcooked task with an AI teammate and, in some rounds, without the AI teammate. After each episode, you will answer two short questions about your experience.

Voluntary participation and withdrawal

Participation is voluntary. You may withdraw from the study at any time without giving a reason and without negative consequences. If you withdraw, no new data will be collected from you. Data collected up to the point of withdrawal may still be used in the research unless you request otherwise, where this is possible.

Data collected during the study

The study collects your participant ID, age, gender, education level, video game experience, AI experience, previous Overcooked experience, consent status, task performance, actions during gameplay, selected AI policies, timing information, and post-episode ratings. Your name or email address should not be entered into the experiment interface.

Confidentiality and data use

Your data will be used for research purposes only. The experimental data will be stored using your participant ID rather than your name. Information needed for compensation will be handled separately from the analysis data where possible. Results will be reported at group level, and individual participants will not be identified in publications, presentations, or thesis work.

Compensation

Participants will receive a voucher as compensation according to Aalto University compensation criteria. Compensation is based on participation and following the study instructions, not on game score. Participants who do not pass the instruction and comprehension check will not continue to the main experiment.

PART 1: Play smart not hard!

You are a chef working with an AI teammate in a busy kitchen. Your goal is not just to move fast: your goal is to help your team get the highest possible score.

How scoring works

  • You gain points when dishes are served.
  • You lose points for every step you take.

This means the best strategy is usually to work efficiently: help serve dishes while avoiding unnecessary movement.

Try to coordinate with your AI teammate instead of doing everything by yourself.
